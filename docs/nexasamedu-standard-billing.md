| Charge | Standard rate | Founding-school rate |

The founding-school rate includes a 20% discount and applies only during the school's first academic session, covering a maximum of three academic terms. At the end of the founding period, the prevailing standard subscription rate will apply.

## 4. Implementation And Onboarding

The one-time implementation fee includes:

- School account configuration and branding
- Initial student and staff data migration using the approved import format
- Academic session, term, class, arm, subject, and assessment setup
- Configuration of one standard report-card template
- Initial roles and permissions configuration
- Remote training for designated administrators and teachers
- Deployment, go-live assistance, and initial implementation support

Complex historical-data migration, data cleaning, custom integrations, and additional report-card templates will be assessed and quoted separately.

## 5. Included Subscription Services

The subscription includes access to the features available under the subscribed NexasamEdu academic and reporting package, including:

- Student and staff records
- Academic structure and subject management
- Teacher, class-arm, and subject assignments
- Role-based access and permission controls
- Configurable assessment structures
- Manual and spreadsheet-based score entry
- Score locking, review, and approval workflows
- Independent-term and cumulative result processing
- Report-card preparation and PDF generation
- Class broadsheets and academic exports
- School branding and hosted school portal
- Platform updates, routine backups, and remote technical support

Future modules may be offered under the existing package or as separately priced add-ons, depending on their scope.

## 6. Student Count And Billing Rules

1. An active student is a student enrolled in the applicable academic session and not marked as withdrawn, graduated, or archived before the term's census date.
2. The school and NexasamEdu will confirm the active-student count on an agreed census date at the beginning of each term.
3. The verified active-student count will form the basis of the term's invoice.
4. Students added before the census date will be included in the current-term invoice.
5. Students admitted after the census date may be carried into the next billing cycle unless the increase is material or otherwise agreed in writing.
6. Withdrawal, suspension, or archival after the census date will not result in a refund or credit for that term.
7. The founding-school discount is non-transferable and applies only to the school named in this quotation.

## 7. Payment Terms

- The implementation fee is payable 70% on acceptance and 30% before production go-live.
- Termly subscription invoices are payable before activation of the applicable academic term.
- A school paying for all three terms in advance will be invoiced using the verified opening student count. Material enrolment increases may be reconciled in subsequent terms.
- Quoted amounts exclude applicable taxes unless expressly stated otherwise.
- Payment-provider, bank, or transfer charges are borne by the paying party.
- Overdue invoices may lead to suspension of write access after reasonable notice. The school will retain an opportunity to export its data subject to the agreement and applicable obligations.

## 8. Exclusions And Optional Services

The following are not included unless expressly stated in the accepted quotation:

- SMS, WhatsApp, and third-party messaging charges
- Payment-gateway and financial-transaction charges
- Custom domain registration or renewal
- On-site training, travel, accommodation, or logistics
- Manual data-entry services outside the agreed migration scope
- Bespoke integrations and school-specific feature development
- Additional or highly customised report-card templates
- Hardware, biometric devices, identity cards, or networking equipment

Optional services will be quoted and approved before work begins.

## 9. Renewal And Price Review

The founding-school discount expires after the first academic session or three academic terms, whichever occurs first. Renewal will be based on the prevailing standard rate, currently N1,500 per active student per term.

NexasamEdu may review its standard pricing to reflect inflation, exchange-rate movements, infrastructure costs, taxes, or material product expansion. The school will receive advance notice before a revised price applies to a new billing period.
