# The Alert - Character and Location Bible

## Global Continuity Rules

- Photorealistic contemporary Nigerian drama
- Natural skin texture and restrained expressions
- No beauty-filter skin, fashion-editorial posing, or luxury styling
- Early-evening warm practical light with subtle cool phone illumination
- Wardrobe, hair, facial hair, and accessories must remain identical across every shot
- Do not add watches, jewellery, tattoos, glasses, or hats unless defined below

## Daniel Adebayo

### Identity

- Nigerian Yoruba man, age 31
- Medium-dark brown skin
- Lean, average-height build
- Oval face, thoughtful dark-brown eyes
- Short low-cut black hair with a clean natural hairline
- Neatly trimmed short beard and moustache
- Calm physical presence; tension appears through his jaw and hands

### Wardrobe

- Muted olive-green crew-neck T-shirt
- Dark charcoal trousers
- No shoes inside the apartment
- No jewellery or wristwatch

### Character Behaviour

- Speaks quietly
- Rarely gestures broadly
- Holds the phone with both hands when anxious
- Looks away before difficult statements
- Does not cry; emotion remains contained

### Reference Views Required

- Neutral front portrait
- Left and right three-quarter portraits
- Full-body front view
- Seated profile holding a generic smartphone
- Concerned expression
- Resolved but not smiling expression

## Femi Cole

### Identity

- Nigerian man, age 32
- Warm deep-brown skin
- Slightly stocky, average-height build
- Broad expressive face and dark-brown eyes
- Short black fade haircut
- Clean-shaven face
- Open, familiar body language

### Wardrobe

- Faded rust-red short-sleeve button-up shirt, worn open
- Plain off-white T-shirt underneath
- Dark blue jeans
- Simple black slip-on sandals
- No jewellery or wristwatch

### Character Behaviour

- Uses humour to soften serious statements
- Leans forward when persuading Daniel
- Handles apartment objects casually because he is a trusted friend
- Concern is visible beneath his confidence
- Final smile is reluctant and affectionate, not defeated

### Reference Views Required

- Neutral front portrait
- Left and right three-quarter portraits
- Full-body front view
- Seated profile with a takeaway container
- Persuasive expression
- Quiet, reflective expression

## Daniel's Apartment

### Layout

- Small Lagos one-bedroom apartment living area
- Compact two-seat charcoal fabric sofa against a warm off-white wall
- Small rectangular wooden dining table with two mismatched chairs
- Narrow window with simple beige curtains
- Modest open shelf with a few books and household items
- Small router with one flickering indicator light
- Scuffed but clean tiled floor
- Entrance door visible in the background

### Hero Props

- Generic black smartphone with no visible brand
- Cream overdue-rent notice with no readable address
- Closed, well-used dark-brown Bible
- Two plain takeaway containers
- Older black laptop with one corner repaired using black tape
- Water bottle and two simple cups

### Lighting

- Early-evening ambience through the window
- One warm ceiling fixture or standing lamp
- Cool phone light may illuminate Daniel's face in close-ups
- No dramatic coloured lights, neon signs, candles, or luxury fixtures

### Spatial Continuity

- Sofa is frame-left when looking from the dining area
- Dining table is frame-right
- Window is behind and slightly left of the sofa
- Entrance door is deep background frame-right
- Rent notice, Bible, and phone begin together on the table beside the sofa

## Negative Prompt Shared Across Assets

Avoid: extra people, changing clothes, changing hairstyles, jewellery, watches, eyeglasses, duplicated limbs, distorted hands, unreadable facial features, luxury apartment, church interior, visible bank logos, visible account details, oversaturated colours, glamour lighting, excessive crying, broad comedy, text overlays, watermarks.
