# The Alert: Shot List and AI Storyboard

## Production Rules

- Master format: 1920x1080, 24 fps, 16:9. Keep faces and key props inside the central 60% for a later 9:16 crop.
- Generate shots without spoken audio. Record dialogue separately and use reaction angles, profiles, inserts, and off-camera lines to reduce lip-sync dependence.
- Use the Daniel, Femi, and apartment reference images for every generated shot in which they appear.
- Keep the same late-afternoon light direction: window light enters from camera-left.
- Phone screens in shots 3, 6, 11, and 22 are blank/green during generation and replaced with designed screen graphics in the edit.
- Generate 5-8 seconds per shot, even when only 2-4 seconds will appear in the final cut.

## Shared Prompt Base

Append this to every generative prompt:

> Grounded contemporary Nigerian drama in the exact modest Lagos apartment from the location reference. Photorealistic 35mm cinema, natural skin texture, restrained warm-neutral grade, soft late-afternoon window light from camera-left, subtle handheld stability, realistic motion, no glamour lighting. Preserve the supplied character's exact face, body, hair, wardrobe, and age. No text, subtitles, logos, extra people, wardrobe changes, facial drift, distorted hands, duplicated objects, or camera-facing performance.

## Phone Composites

Design all phone graphics in Figma, Canva, or Resolve Fusion and track them onto a blank screen. Use a fictional bank name and hide account numbers. The sequence needs only:

1. Credit alert for NGN 2,400,000.
2. Sender message saying the transfer was made in error.
3. Generic transfer screen under Daniel's hovering thumb.
4. Bank case confirmation showing that the disputed funds are restricted.

## Coverage Safety

Generate or film three extra neutral reactions: Daniel thinking, Femi listening, and the phone face down. These 4-6 second clips will hide dialogue edits, continuity errors, and failed lip sync.
