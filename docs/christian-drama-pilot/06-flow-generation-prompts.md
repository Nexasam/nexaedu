# The Alert - Google Flow Generation Prompts

Shot 1 is approved. Generate the remaining clips one at a time. For every shot, choose **Video**, **16:9**, **8 seconds**, and **two outputs** where credits permit.

## Reference Setup

Keep these assets in the Flow project:

- `Daniel`: `reference-assets/flow-ingredients/daniel.png`
- `Femi`: `reference-assets/flow-ingredients/femi.png`
- `Apartment`: `reference-assets/flow-ingredients/apartment.png`
- `Shot 1 approved`: `production/shot-01-approved.mp4`, or a saved frame from it

Use only the ingredients named for each shot. Too many unrelated references can cause identity mixing.

## Continuity Lock

Append this block to every prompt:

```text
Match the approved Shot 1 visual style and apartment exactly. Warm late-afternoon light enters from the barred window on camera-left. Grounded contemporary Nigerian drama, photorealistic 35mm cinema, realistic skin texture, restrained warm-neutral colour grade, subtle natural movement.

Daniel is exclusively the lean man in the olive-green T-shirt, charcoal trousers, and black wristwatch. Femi is exclusively the stockier man in the open rust-red shirt, cream T-shirt, dark jeans, and brown bracelet. Never exchange, blend, or duplicate their faces, bodies, or wardrobes.

No dialogue or lip-sync performance. No subtitles, readable text, logos, extra people, duplicated characters, wardrobe changes, distorted hands, malformed props, exaggerated acting, or looking into the camera. Keep useful action inside the central area of the 16:9 frame.
```

## Selection Rule

Approve a generation only when:

1. Daniel and Femi retain their correct faces, builds, and wardrobes.
2. The window remains left, the sofa remains central, and the chair remains right.
3. Props do not multiply or move without story motivation.
4. Hands are credible throughout the usable action.
5. The performance is understated and contains no visible speech.
6. At least 3-5 seconds of the clip are clean enough for the final edit.

Save approved downloads using `shot-02.mp4`, `shot-03.mp4`, and so forth. Keep rejected variations until the sequence is assembled in case a reaction or insert becomes useful elsewhere.
