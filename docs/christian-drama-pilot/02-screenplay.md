# The Alert - Screenplay

**Estimated runtime:** 3 minutes  
**Location:** Daniel's apartment, Lagos  
**Time:** Early evening

## Characters

- **Daniel Adebayo, 31:** A quiet, capable IT support specialist between jobs. His faith is sincere but currently under financial pressure.
- **Femi Cole, 32:** Daniel's longtime friend. Warm, funny, practical, and also Christian. He wants Daniel to survive and is skilled at making compromise sound reasonable.

## Screenplay

### 00:00-00:18 - The Alert

Close on a phone vibrating beside an overdue rent notice and a closed Bible.

Daniel reaches for it. The blue light from the screen catches his face.

He sits forward.

**DANIEL**  
Femi.

Femi looks up from the takeaway container he is opening at the small dining table.

**FEMI**  
Hmm?

**DANIEL**  
Two point four million.

Silence.

### 00:18-00:42 - The Possibility

Femi crosses to Daniel and looks at the phone without taking it.

**FEMI**  
For the contract?

**DANIEL**  
What contract?

Daniel refreshes the generic banking screen.

**DANIEL**  
I don't know this name.

Femi notices the rent notice.

**FEMI**  
Did you pray this morning?

Daniel gives him a warning look.

**FEMI**  
I am only asking. Sometimes answers arrive before explanations.

### 00:42-01:12 - The Argument

Daniel's phone vibrates again. A message preview reads: **TRANSFER MADE IN ERROR. PLEASE CALL.**

Daniel turns the phone face down.

**DANIEL**  
There is the explanation.

**FEMI**  
Or the test.

**DANIEL**  
Which test?

**FEMI**  
Whether you finally know when to stop suffering.

Femi picks up the rent notice.

**FEMI**  
Three months. Your laptop is dying. Your mother still thinks you have work. Daniel, even banks make mistakes and continue sleeping.

### 01:12-01:43 - The Temptation

Daniel looks around the apartment: the flickering router, repaired chair, nearly empty shelf.

**FEMI**  
Move what you need. If they reverse the rest, fine.

**DANIEL**  
And if they ask?

**FEMI**  
You did not send it to yourself.

**DANIEL**  
That is not an answer.

**FEMI**  
It is the kind of answer people use when rent is due.

Daniel picks up the phone. His thumb hovers over the screen.

### 01:43-02:15 - The Decision

Daniel glances at the closed Bible, then back to the phone. He does not open the Bible.

**DANIEL**  
If I have to hide how God provided, then God did not provide it.

Femi exhales and sits back.

**FEMI**  
So you will send it back to a stranger?

**DANIEL**  
No. That could be another scam.

Daniel opens the bank's support screen.

**DANIEL**  
I will report the transfer and let the bank reverse it properly.

He submits a transaction dispute. A generic confirmation appears: **REPORT RECEIVED. FUNDS RESTRICTED PENDING REVIEW.**

### 02:15-02:43 - The Cost

The room is quiet again.

Femi places the rent notice back on the table.

**FEMI**  
So what now?

Daniel looks at the notice, then locks the phone.

**DANIEL**  
Now I still owe rent.

A beat.

**DANIEL**  
But I do not owe my conscience.

Femi studies him, then gives a reluctant half-smile.

**FEMI**  
Your sermons are expensive.

**DANIEL**  
Truth usually is.

### 02:43-03:00 - End

Femi slides the second takeaway container toward Daniel.

Daniel accepts it. They eat in silence.

The phone remains face down beside the rent notice.

Cut to black.

**ON SCREEN:**  
Integrity is still obedience when it costs you.  
Proverbs 10:9

**END**

## Performance Notes

- Daniel should not sound heroic. He is tired and tempted.
- Femi should speak from concern, not greed.
- Hold silence after the transfer amount, after the dispute confirmation, and before Daniel's final answer.
- The final shared meal communicates friendship without making Femi deliver a sudden conversion speech.
