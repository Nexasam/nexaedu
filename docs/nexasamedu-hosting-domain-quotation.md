## 1. Purpose

This quotation covers the initial production infrastructure required to deploy NexasamEdu for its first school pilot and early commercial operation.

A new Namecheap Stellar Plus hosting subscription will be purchased specifically for NexasamEdu. It may be managed from the existing Namecheap customer dashboard, but it will have a separate cPanel environment, credentials, databases, application files, backups, and hosting-resource allocation from the existing affiliate SaaS project.

## 2. Annual Infrastructure Provision

| Description | Annual provision |
|---|---:|
| Dedicated Namecheap Stellar Plus hosting subscription | N116,000 |
| NexasamEdu domain registration or renewal | N20,000 |
| External backup and storage provision | N10,000 |
| Exchange-rate, payment, and unforeseen-cost provision | N24,000 |
| **Total annual infrastructure provision** | **N170,000** |

The amounts above are budgetary provisions based on current provider prices and exchange rates. Actual provider invoices and receipts will be retained for reconciliation. Any unused contingency will remain allocated to NexasamEdu infrastructure and will be reviewed at renewal.

## 3. Included Infrastructure

The annual provision includes:

- A separate Namecheap Stellar Plus hosting subscription
- A separate cPanel environment for NexasamEdu
- MySQL database hosting
- Application and school-portal hosting
- School subdomain support
- SSL certificate provisioning
- NexasamEdu domain registration or renewal
- Hosting email accounts, subject to provider limits
- Routine hosting backups
- External database and file-backup provision
- Initial DNS and domain configuration
- Initial production deployment environment
- Basic infrastructure monitoring and maintenance
- Allowance for exchange-rate and provider-price movements

## 4. Deployment Arrangement

The existing Stellar Plus subscription associated with `emiplug.com` will continue to host the affiliate SaaS project.

NexasamEdu will use a newly purchased hosting subscription. The two applications will not share cPanel environments, application files, databases, credentials, backups, or hosting-resource allocations.

This structure provides better operational separation while allowing both subscriptions to be managed from the same Namecheap customer dashboard.

## 5. Payment Terms

- Total payable: **N170,000**
- Billing frequency: **Annual**
- Payment requirement: **100% before infrastructure purchase and deployment**
- The coverage period begins when the hosting subscription and domain are activated.
- Renewal pricing will be reviewed before the end of each 12-month coverage period.
- Material provider-price or exchange-rate changes may require a revised renewal provision.

## 6. Exclusions

The annual infrastructure provision does not include:

- Application design or software-development fees
- School onboarding, migration, or training costs
- Customer-support personnel
- SMS, WhatsApp, or bulk-messaging charges
- Transactional email charges above available free or hosting allowances
- Payment-gateway or financial-transaction charges
- On-site visits, travel, accommodation, or logistics
- Third-party commercial software licences
- Bespoke integrations or feature development
- VPS, dedicated-server, or managed-cloud migration
- Major disaster-recovery or high-availability infrastructure

Excluded services will be presented for approval before any related expenditure is incurred.

## 7. Upgrade And Scaling Policy

The shared-hosting arrangement is intended for the pilot and early commercial stage. Migration to a VPS or managed cloud platform will be reviewed when one or more of the following occurs:

- Three to five schools are actively using NexasamEdu
- Hosting-resource limits are approached consistently
- Concurrent score entry or bulk report generation affects performance
- Parent and student portals create sustained traffic
- Online payment or sensitive financial workflows are introduced
- Persistent queue workers, more frequent scheduled jobs, or dedicated caching become necessary
- Stronger monitoring, availability, or disaster recovery is required

Any major infrastructure upgrade will be budgeted and approved separately.

## 8. Commercial Clarification

This quotation represents an internal NexasamEdu infrastructure provision and is separate from the school-facing subscription quotation.

Hosting for subscribed schools is included in the applicable NexasamEdu subscription fee and should not be billed to a school again as a separate infrastructure charge unless a dedicated or custom hosting arrangement is requested.

## 9. Ownership And Administration

- The hosting subscription, domain, application deployment, and associated infrastructure are business assets used for NexasamEdu operations.
- Administrative credentials will be stored securely and access will be restricted to authorised persons.
- Provider invoices and renewal notices will be made available for business records.
- School data will remain logically separated by the NexasamEdu tenancy controls and protected through the application's access policies.
